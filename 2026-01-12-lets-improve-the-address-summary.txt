  New Features Added:

  1. Role Filtering (--role)

    - sender: Show only addresses that are transaction senders

    - Formatted with thousand separators for readability
    - Only includes capacity for stamps where the address is the owner

  Additional Parameters:

  - --refresh: Refresh balance data from blockchain
  - --max-retries: Maximum retries for rate-limited requests
  - --cache-validity-blocks: Cache validity duration
